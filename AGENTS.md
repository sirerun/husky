# Husky repository instructions

- Follow `docs/design.md` and `docs/adr/` for accepted product and architecture decisions; update a decision record when those decisions change.
- Keep Husky a generic typed-chat client. Do not add microphone capture, transcription, a hosted assistant backend, or public tool execution.
- Keep public files free of secrets, private local paths, customer data, ignored scratch artifacts, and unqualified third-party assets.
- Tie behavior and release claims to recorded verification; distinguish fixture, local native, CI, signed-release, install, and automatic-update evidence.
